(01)About ProjectBaluga

Technology that fits your business

ProjectBaluga is an independent technology service focused on practical solutions for small businesses. The goal is simple: understand how your business works and help make its everyday processes easier to manage.

From business software and network configuration to focused automation, each engagement starts with a conversation about your needs, constraints, and priorities. Recommendations are based on the problem to solve—not on adding technology for its own sake.

Work is scoped with clear expectations before implementation. Where a project requires specialist services, hardware, or ongoing support, those requirements are discussed up front.

(02) Services

Solutions for real work

Straightforward technology services for small businesses. We begin by understanding the work you need to improve, then agree on a practical scope, timeline, and next steps.

Custom Business Systems

Replace scattered spreadsheets and repetitive admin work with software shaped around how your business operates.

  • Inventory, sales, billing, and customer records
  • Loan, payment, and account workflows
  • Reports, dashboards, and role-based access

Good fit: small businesses, shops, lending teams, and service providers.

Network & MikroTik Solutions

Make network services easier to operate, monitor, and support with practical configuration and purpose-built tools.

  • Hotspot and voucher workflows
  • MikroTik and network-service integration
  • Authentication, remote access, and troubleshooting

Good fit: ISPs, internet cafés, resorts, and organizations with managed networks.

Custom Software & Automation

Connect the tools and infrastructure your team depends on, or build a focused application for a specialized workflow.

  • Internal dashboards, APIs, and desktop applications
  • Windows Server and infrastructure automation
  • System integration, deployment, and documentation

Good fit: teams with repetitive processes or specialized technical requirements.
