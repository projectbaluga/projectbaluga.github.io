PROJECTBALUGA / PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This notice explains what information may be handled when you use the ProjectBaluga website, submit a project inquiry, or message the website's AI assistant.

1. Information you provide

If you submit the project inquiry form, the information may include your name, email address, business name, contact number, selected service, project description, estimated budget, and target timeline.

If you use the AI assistant, the message text you send is transmitted to the ProjectBaluga AI service so it can generate a response. Please do not enter passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, confidential business records, or other sensitive information.

2. How inquiry information is processed

The inquiry form uses FormSubmit as a third-party form delivery service. Information you submit is sent to FormSubmit and forwarded to ProjectBaluga at projectbaluga@gmail.com so we can review and respond to your request. FormSubmit's published help page says it retains submissions for 30 days; copies delivered to the ProjectBaluga email inbox may remain separately until they are deleted.

For more information, review FormSubmit's help page and its published privacy document.

3. AI assistant messages

Messages sent to the AI assistant are processed by the ProjectBaluga Cloudflare Worker and Cloudflare Workers AI to generate a reply. The website code does not save conversation history to a ProjectBaluga database; conversation history is kept in the active page session. This does not mean the service provider never processes technical or request data. Please review Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for information about its handling of data.

4. How information is used

ProjectBaluga uses inquiry details to understand your request, contact you, prepare an appropriate response, and discuss a possible project. AI assistant messages are used to provide the requested automated response. We do not ask visitors to submit sensitive credentials or payment information through these features.

5. Retention and requests

Inquiry emails may be kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond to the inquiry, maintain relevant business correspondence, or meet applicable obligations. Copies in third-party services may be subject to those providers' own retention practices. You may contact ProjectBaluga to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable requirements and any copies that must be retained.

6. Website technology

The website uses browser local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests font files from Google. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. These providers may process technical information as part of delivering their services; consult their privacy documentation for details.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about an inquiry you submitted, email projectbaluga@gmail.com.

This notice describes the current website implementation and known service providers. It is not a legal opinion. Review and update it if the website's services, providers, or data practices change.
